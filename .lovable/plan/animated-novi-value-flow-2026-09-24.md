# Animated NOVI Value Flow

## Goal
Show convincingly how NOVI turns fragmented idea material into evidence-based decisions and a board-ready business case.

## Build
- Create one reusable bilingual flow component in the selected kinetic style.
- Add a compact version near the top of the Feature Overview.
- Replace the simple process strip in the Tool Guide with a detailed version.
- Show inputs, all seven phases, five decision gates, IDA support, the verification feedback loop, and final outputs.
- Use moving signals and progressive emphasis, with motion disabled when users prefer reduced motion.
- Reflow into a clear vertical journey on mobile.

## Content
- Inputs: idea, attachments, scan results, interviews, internal knowledge.
- Process: scoring, market intelligence, TAM/SAM/SOM, verification, business case, GTM, implementation review.
- Decisions: G1–G5 with Go, Hold, and Stop discipline.
- Outputs: evidence trail, calibrated market model, financial case, recommendation, complete PDF.

## Validation
- Check both pages in German and English.
- Verify desktop and mobile layouts, animations, text fit, and reduced-motion behavior.
